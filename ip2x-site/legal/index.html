The principle behind all of it

Your identity and your exercise data are kept apart.

IP2X is built so that Personal Data lives separately from pseudonymised Exercise Data, and only IP2X holds the key that links them. Research partners never receive anything that identifies you. Commercial partners see only what their own programme requires. We do not sell personal data or identifiable health data.
