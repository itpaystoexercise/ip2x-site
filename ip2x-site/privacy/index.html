Privacy Notice

What we collect, how we use it, and who we share it with.

← All legal documents

Draft version 1.0. This document is a working draft and is not yet in force. It is published here for review and will change before the IP2X app launches. Questions to hello@ip2x.com.

1Introduction

IP2X Limited ("IP2X", "we", "our" or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information responsibly.

This Privacy Notice explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices available to you.

We have designed IP2X around the principle of privacy by design. Wherever possible, information that identifies you is stored separately from information relating to your exercise activity.

2Who We Are

IP2X is the data controller for the personal information described in this Privacy Notice, except where we process information solely on behalf of a Commercial Partner. In those circumstances, IP2X may act as a data processor in accordance with applicable data protection law.

3The Information We Collect

To operate the Services, IP2X maintains three separate categories of information.

A. Personal Data

This information identifies you and is stored within the client-facing database.

It may include:

  • name;
  • date of birth;
  • email address;
  • telephone number;
  • postal address (where required);
  • login credentials;
  • communication preferences;
  • avatar selection;
  • exercise preferences (for example, gym or home);
  • account settings.

You may review and update most Personal Data through the IP2X app or website using two-factor authentication.

B. Exercise Data

Exercise Data is stored separately from Personal Data using pseudonymisation.

Exercise Data may include:

  • exercise plans;
  • completed exercise sessions;
  • timestamps;
  • exercise duration;
  • motion sensor information;
  • wearable device measurements;
  • exercise validation results;
  • behavioural indicators;
  • responses to exercise questions;
  • physiological measurements connected to exercise.

Exercise Data does not normally contain information that directly identifies you.

Only IP2X retains the secure information required to link Exercise Data with your Personal Data.

C. Commercial Partner Data

Commercial Partners may provide information including:

  • policy numbers;
  • employee identifiers;
  • NHS numbers;
  • customer reference numbers;
  • programme identifiers;
  • limited programme information.

This information is stored separately from both Personal Data and Exercise Data.

4How We Use Your Information

We use Personal Data to:

  • create and manage your account;
  • authenticate your identity;
  • communicate with you;
  • administer programmes;
  • manage Rewards;
  • provide customer support;
  • comply with legal obligations.

We use Exercise Data to:

  • verify exercise completion;
  • generate exercise reports;
  • administer reward programmes;
  • improve our Services;
  • develop exercise verification technologies;
  • support approved research using pseudonymised information.

We use Commercial Partner Data to:

  • match existing Clients;
  • invite eligible individuals to join IP2X where authorised;
  • administer partner programmes;
  • support white-labelled implementations.

5Sharing Your Information

Commercial Partners

Personal Data may be shared with the relevant Commercial Partner where necessary to:

  • match Clients to partner records;
  • administer programmes;
  • confirm eligibility;
  • administer Rewards;
  • verify participation.

Commercial Partners cannot amend your Personal Data within IP2X.

Exercise reports relating to their own programmes may also be shared with the relevant Commercial Partner.

Commercial Partners cannot access Exercise Data outside the reports authorised for their own programmes.

Research Partners

Approved Research Partners may receive pseudonymised Exercise Data.

Research Partners:

  • do not receive Personal Data;
  • are not provided with the information required to identify individuals;
  • are contractually prohibited from attempting to identify any individual;
  • may not contact Clients;
  • may only publish aggregated findings.

Healthcare Professionals

IP2X will not share your identifiable Health Data with your GP, specialist, surgeon or any other healthcare professional unless:

  • you instruct us to do so; or
  • we are legally required to do so.

Legal Requirements

We may disclose information where required by law, court order or a lawful request from a regulatory or public authority.

6Sale of Information

IP2X does not sell your Personal Data.

IP2X does not sell identifiable Health Data.

From time to time, organisations may wish to obtain anonymised or aggregated exercise information for purposes such as public health research, policy development or sports participation analysis.

Where your consent is legally required, IP2X will request your explicit opt-in permission before sharing information beyond the operation of the Services.

7Our Privacy Architecture

Protecting your privacy is central to the design of IP2X.

Accordingly:

  • Personal Data and Exercise Data are stored separately.
  • Exercise Data is pseudonymised.
  • Only IP2X controls the information capable of linking your identity to Exercise Data.
  • Research Partners cannot access Personal Data.
  • Commercial Partners cannot access research datasets.
  • Revenue earned from different Commercial Partners is never disclosed between those partners.

8How Long We Keep Information

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • provide the Services;
  • comply with legal obligations;
  • resolve disputes;
  • enforce our agreements.

Where information is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

9Security

IP2X uses technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

These measures include, where appropriate:

  • encryption;
  • secure cloud infrastructure;
  • access controls;
  • role-based permissions;
  • audit logging;
  • two-factor authentication;
  • regular security monitoring.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security, but IP2X continually reviews and improves its security controls.

10Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • access your Personal Data;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • request deletion of information;
  • restrict processing;
  • object to certain processing;
  • receive a copy of your information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where processing relies on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Some rights may be limited where legal exemptions apply.

11Cookies and Analytics

The IP2X website and app may use cookies and similar technologies to:

  • remember your preferences;
  • maintain secure sessions;
  • understand platform performance;
  • improve user experience.

Where required by law, you will be asked for your consent before non-essential cookies are used.

12Changes to this Privacy Notice

We may update this Privacy Notice from time to time.

Material changes will be notified through the app, website or by email where appropriate.

The latest version will always be available through the IP2X website.

13Contact Us

Questions regarding this Privacy Notice or the way IP2X handles personal information should be directed to:

Email: hello@ip2x.com
