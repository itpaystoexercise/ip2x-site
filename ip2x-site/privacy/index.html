Privacy

Your health data belongs to you.

This policy explains what we collect, what we do with it, and — just as importantly — what we never share.

Draft — pending legal review. This policy describes how IP2X intends to handle personal data. It has not yet been reviewed by our solicitors and may change before the IP2X app launches. If anything here matters to a decision you're making, please email hello@ip2x.com and ask.

Last updated: July 2026

The short version

Your health and exercise data is yours. When you nominate a commercial partner — an insurer, employer or healthcare provider — we send them a simple yes or no answer to one question: did you meet your monthly exercise target? Nothing else. We do not tell them what you did, or when you did it.

We will never share your health data with anyone else unless you instruct us to.

Who we are

IP2X ("It Pays To Exercise") is registered in England, company number 17151019. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at hello@ip2x.com.

What we collect

  • Account details — your name, email address and the login credentials you create.
  • Onboarding answers — age, current fitness level, goals, relevant health history, and whether you exercise at home or at a gym. We use these to build your Personal Exercise Plan.
  • Exercise and wearable data — activity recorded by the wearable you choose to connect, and the sessions you complete in the app.
  • Your nominated partners — which insurers, employers or healthcare providers you have asked us to report to.

Some of this is health data, which UK data protection law treats as a special category. We process it on the basis of your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

What we share, and with whom

For each commercial partner you nominate, and only for as long as you keep them nominated, we send a monthly confirmation of whether you met the exercise requirement. That confirmation is binary — met, or not met.

We do not send partners your workouts, your heart rate, your weight, your medical history, or the dates and times you exercised. If you stop nominating a partner, we stop reporting to them.

Healthcare providers are a special case: where you have been referred by a GP, surgeon or clinical team, you can choose to share fuller progress reports with that clinician. That sharing is yours to switch on and off.

Wearables

IP2X is wearable agnostic — Apple, Garmin, Fitbit, Samsung and others. When you connect a device, you authorise us to read activity data from it. You can disconnect it at any time, and we will stop reading new data immediately.

This website

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and does not track you. It does load fonts from Google Fonts, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads — that request is made by your browser, not by us, and we receive nothing from it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and exercise data for as long as your account is open, and for a limited period afterwards where we are required to for legal or accounting reasons. Ask us to delete your account and we will delete your personal data.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it erased, to receive a copy in a portable format, to object to or restrict how we process it, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email hello@ip2x.com and we will respond within one month.

If you think we have handled your data badly, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. We would rather you told us first so we can put it right.

Changes to this policy

If we make a material change we will tell account holders by email before it takes effect.
