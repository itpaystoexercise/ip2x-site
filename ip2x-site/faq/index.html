Getting started

Download the IP2X app, answer a few quick questions about yourself and we'll create your free Personal Exercise Plan. No gym membership or equipment needed beyond your phone and a wearable device.

You'll need a wearable to get the full IP2X experience, including earning your proof of exercise. You can pick one up for under £40 — or sign up with one of our partners (including the NHS) and you may be able to get one at a discount.

Your plan

We look at your age, fitness level, body type and goals to set your starting point and pace. After every set, we ask how it felt — too much, about right or could do more. We combine that with your wearable data to gently adjust your plan each time.

Two 30-minute sessions a week is the core plan. We'll also track any other exercise you do — walks, runs, whatever you enjoy — so nothing goes unrecorded.

Not necessarily. Each session is designed as a full circuit, but you can pause or stop at any time. The important thing is that you complete both elements during your weekly sessions.

Your data

We use your data only to refine your Personal Exercise Plan. We'll share it with your doctor only if you tell us to. Your data belongs to you — we never sell it to any third party.

We collect continuous data including heart rate and respiration rate. We also use blood pressure, SPO2 and heart rate variability where available. We use this to guide you safely during exercise and to produce your verified proof of exercise certificate. We also collect motion-sensing data from the wearable for the proof of exercise.
