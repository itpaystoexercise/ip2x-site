1. Who we are

2. What IP2X gives you

We provide a Personal Exercise Plan, guidance through each session, tracking via a wearable device you connect, and verified monthly proof that you have exercised. IP2X is free to individual users. It always will be.

3. Rewards from commercial partners

Rewards — cash, discounts, premium reductions or other benefits — are offered by the insurers, employers, healthcare providers and vendors you nominate. They are not offered by IP2X. Each partner sets its own targets, its own reward and its own conditions, and can change or withdraw them.

What we do is verify, honestly and monthly, whether you met the target. Whether a reward is then paid is a matter between you and that partner.

4. Your account

You need to be 18 or over to use IP2X. Keep your login details to yourself, give us accurate information when you sign up, and don't attempt to falsify exercise data — verification is the whole point of the product, and we may suspend or close accounts where data has been manipulated.

5. Health and safety — please read this one

IP2X is not a medical service and does not provide medical advice. Our plans are built by qualified personal trainers, not by your doctor, and they are not a substitute for professional medical care.

Talk to a doctor before starting any new exercise programme, particularly if you have an existing condition, are pregnant, are recovering from surgery or illness, or are taking prescribed medication. Stop exercising and seek medical help if you feel unwell.

You exercise at your own risk. Nothing in these terms limits our liability for death or personal injury caused by our negligence, for fraud, or for anything else that cannot be limited under English law.

We are not responsible for hardware faults, sync failures or changes third parties make to their own services, though we will do what we reasonably can to help.
