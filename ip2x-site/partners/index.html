Reporting

Reporting is what sets us apart.

Whatever you're rewarding, you see exactly what's happening. Verified, auditable reporting is built into IP2X from day one — not bolted on.

  • Verified monthly reports — Proof of Exercise for every member or employee, confirming the monthly requirement has been met.
  • Cohort dashboards — participation, adherence and progress across your whole membership or workforce.
  • Auditable records — hard data on verified outcomes, never self-reported estimates.
