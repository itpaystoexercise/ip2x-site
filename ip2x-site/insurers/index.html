The business case

Life and health insurers — two different cases.

These are two very different target markets, so IP2X makes a different case to each. Two health metrics used by UK public bodies frame both: Healthy Life Expectancy (HLE), used to assess the nation's health and the demands on health services, and Quality Adjusted Life Years (QALYs), used to help decide whether medical interventions should be funded by the NHS.

For life insurers

Improved longevity reduces underwriting costs.

Longer policy revenue

Healthier policyholders live longer — paying premiums over extended periods before any claim is triggered.

Lower mortality risk

Industry data points to materially lower mortality risk among consistently active members across all age groups.

Better risk pool

Wellness products attract a healthier cohort of applicants — a natural screening for preferred rates, and higher Healthy Life Expectancy across your book.

For health insurers

Fewer claims, later — and lower care costs.

Fewer, later claims

Reduces the frequency and timing of health claims. Healthier lifestyles delay the onset of chronic disease.

74% preventable

Chronic diseases account for the majority of global deaths — but are largely preventable with consistent exercise.

More quality years

Every healthy year added is a Quality Adjusted Life Year (QALY) — gained at a fraction of the cost of most medical interventions.

  • Portfolio dashboards — participation, adherence and progress across your whole book.
  • Auditable records — hard data your actuarial and underwriting teams can rely on.
How it works for insurers

It pays to be fit — for both sides.

By linking your policyholders to an IP2X plan, you can ensure participating clients are meeting their fitness goals — with verified proof to back it up. Healthier customers use fewer high-cost services. A fitter insured population means fewer sudden, catastrophic medical events and a slower progression of chronic illness. Because claims are the largest component of any insurer's outgoings, a healthier risk pool directly improves your loss ratios — and your profitability.
